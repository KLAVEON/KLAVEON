The first stage checks whether what you know about your brand and what the evidence shows are the same thing. Most founders are surprised by what it surfaces. It takes two weeks and produces the foundation everything else is built on, so it stays in every engagement.

Verbal identity starts once the Defining Idea is approved, and visual identity starts once verbal identity is approved. Running stages at once produces identity systems where the visual direction contradicts the strategic direction, and the rework costs more than the time saved. The sequence is the efficiency.

Two rounds at each stage. A round is one consolidated set of feedback, in a single document or session, that KLAVEON works through before the next round begins. Feedback that arrives in installments counts as more than one round.

Keystone and Signature run in three payments: 50% on signing, 25% when the strategy is approved, and 25% on final delivery. Prospect is paid in full on signing and is delivered within two weeks.

Ownership of every deliverable transfers to you on receipt of final payment. KLAVEON may show completed work in its portfolio, unless you ask for confidentiality in writing before signing.

Scope flexes. The price for the same scope does not. If Signature is outside what you can invest right now, Keystone may be the right place to begin.
