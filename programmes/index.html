Prospect

01

(Direction) · 2 weeks

A written audit of where your brand stands: what it communicates, what the category is doing, and what needs to happen before design begins.

  • Brand Honesty Brief
  • Category position audit
  • What is true versus what the brand claims
  • What must happen before design begins
  • Delivered as a written document

For founders who need a clear picture before committing to anything, and as the diagnostic before any rebrand decision.
