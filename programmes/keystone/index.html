(Fit)

Put a foundation under what already exists.

Keystone is for founders who have a visual identity and no strategy underneath it, for businesses preparing for visual identity work, and for leadership teams that need to agree on the brand before it is expressed outside.

Keystone suits you when:

  • The brand looks professional and is hard to explain in one sentence.
  • The messaging changes between channels and between people.
  • Leaders describe the brand in different words.
  • A visual identity project is coming and the strategy has not been settled.
