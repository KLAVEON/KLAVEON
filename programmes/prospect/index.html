(Fit)

Know what is true before you decide what to build.

Prospect is the first stage of every KLAVEON engagement. It is also sold on its own, for founders who are not ready to commit to a full engagement and want an honest picture of the brand and the category around it.

Prospect suits you when:

  • A rebrand is under discussion and you want evidence before deciding.
  • You are not sure the brand says what you believe it says.
  • You want an outside reading before you commit to a larger engagement.
  • You need to know what has to change before any design starts.
