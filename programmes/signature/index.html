Build the brand from the idea outward.

Signature is for new brands built from scratch and for established brands repositioning from a new strategic direction. It runs from the first reading of the terrain to a usable identity system.

Signature suits you when:

  • You are launching a brand and nothing has been built yet.
  • The business has changed and the brand has not.
  • You want strategy, voice and visuals to come from one source.
  • You want guidelines your team will use, and not file away.

Questions.

The visual identity expresses an idea that has to exist first. When the stages run together, the visuals tend to contradict the strategy, and correcting that costs more than the time saved. The sequence is what makes the work faster in the end.

The timeline depends on the sequence and on how quickly feedback arrives. Delays on the client side extend the timeline, not the scope.
