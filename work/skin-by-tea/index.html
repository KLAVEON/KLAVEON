SKIN by Tea

(01)Overview

We're not just a skincare brand, we're a wellness ritual. SKIN by Tea bridges beauty and wellness by creating herbal teas infused with botanicals that make your skin glow from the inside out.

(02)Challenge

Beauty and wellness is crowded, with many brands making claims about glow, ingestibles and supplements. Few unify drinkable and beauty-first and community-driven in a distinct voice that resonates in Nigeria and globally.

The category often feels either clinical, cold, fluffy or vague. SKIN by Tea had to own a space that feels credible, luminous and sociable.

As a new entrant, it had to articulate a clear identity early (naming, positioning, visual voice) so it would not become just another wellness tea.

(03)Insight

Skincare brands tend to sound clinical and cold. Wellness brands tend to sound fluffy and vague. Neither sounds like a moment a person would want to share.

Tea sits in a different place. It is already a pause, and it is already social. A drink that supports skin can be presented as a ritual, which is warmer than a regimen and easier to repeat with other people.

That gave SKIN by Tea its position: beauty first, drinkable, and community-driven, in a voice that feels credible, luminous and sociable. The product is the moment, and the identity had to look and sound like one.

(04)Idea

Flag / Big Idea: Beauty in the Moment. Every sip is a ritual moment that ripples outward: Pause, Sip, Radiate.

Purpose: To transform skincare into a ritual you sip, empowering radiant skin from within.

Brand vision: A world where beautiful skin begins with wellness rituals that delight, connect and uplift.

Promise: When you drink SKIN by Tea, your skin glows.

(05)Deliverables
  • Logo: horizontal and stacked lockups, monochrome versions, logomark and favicon, co-branding lockup, prohibited use
  • Colour: Mint Pop #99E4D2, Lilac Pop #CA9FE2, Sun Glow #FFD74D, Coral Pop #FF6F61, off-white #F4F4EF, black, and two gradients
  • Typography: a serif primary typeface and Haas Grotesk Display as secondary, with a typographic hierarchy
  • Digital application: website and social media
  • Stationery: business card and event tag
  • Packaging: vial and gift box
  • Photography direction
(06)KLAVEON Reflection

The Drinkable Brand Guide runs to 24 pages, and each one traces back to a single line: Beauty in the Moment. The strategy sets the line, and the system carries it. Four colours named Mint Pop, Lilac Pop, Sun Glow and Coral Pop sit beside two gradients. A serif and Haas Grotesk give the voice two registers. Headlines such as "Drinkable Skincare" and "What's Your Ritual Today?" repeat the strategy in the customer's own language.

The guide also sets limits. A prohibited-use page and a co-branding lockup keep the mark intact when partners, printers and event teams handle it. This is a Signature programme engagement for a B2C brand, delivered in 2025.

The test KLAVEON applies to every system is whether someone could recognise the brand without seeing the logo. This one passes through colour, gradient, type and the habit of the Pause, Sip, Radiate ritual.
